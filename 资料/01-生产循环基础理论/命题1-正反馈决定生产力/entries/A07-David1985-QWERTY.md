# Clio and the Economics of QWERTY
- **作者**：Paul A. David
- **出处**：American Economic Review, vol. 75, no. 2 (Papers and Proceedings), pp. 332-337
- **年份**：1985
- **DOI**：-
- **核心观点**：提出"路径依赖"概念——经济过程中遥远的历史事件（包括随机因素）可以通过正反馈机制锁定最终结果。技术关联性、规模经济和投资的准不可逆性是锁定的三大要素。通过QWERTY键盘案例说明：一个微小的历史偶然可以被正反馈放大为不可逆的标准锁定。
- **与命题的关联**：正面论证。David 证明正反馈不仅驱动增长（加速），也驱动锁定（定性选择）。在命题1语境下：正循环的方向和强度决定了解锁哪种增长路径。
- **摘录/笔记**：
  > "A path-dependent sequence of economic changes is one of which important influences upon the eventual outcome can be exerted by temporally remote events, including happenings dominated by chance elements rather than systematic forces."
  > three features causing QWERTY lock-in: "technical interrelatedness, economies of scale, and quasi-irreversibility of investment."
  > "the initial lead acquired by QWERTY through its association with the Remington was quantitatively very slender, when magnified by expectations it may well have been quite sufficient to guarantee that the industry eventually would lock in to a de facto QWERTY standard."
