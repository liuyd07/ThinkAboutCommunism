# Economics and the Collectively Autocatalytic Structure of the Real Economy / Emergence of Autocatalytic Sets in a Simple Model of Technological Evolution
- **作者**：Stuart Kauffman (2011); Hordijk, Kauffman & Koppl (2023)
- **出处**：NPR 13.7 Cosmos & Culture (2011); Journal of Evolutionary Economics, 33:1519-1535 (2023)
- **年份**：2011 / 2023
- **DOI** (2023)：10.1007/s00191-023-00838-2
- **核心观点**：经济的"技术现实"是一个集体自催化集。生产函数相当于化学反应，产品和工具相当于催化剂。当产品多样性超过临界阈值时，自催化集自发涌现。第一世界经济（supracritical）不断产生新的商品和服务多样性，年增长约2%；第三世界经济（subcritical）无法产生新多样性。自催化集理论说明在相变点附近，小规模的相互催化集可能爆发。
- **与命题的关联**：正面论证核心。这是将自催化集理论从化学/生物学直接应用到经济学的最直接文献。Kauffman 提出整个经济是集体自催化集，其增长=正循环的自我维持和自我扩展。supracritical/subcritical 的区分直接对应命题——是否形成了自我维持的生产正循环决定了增长的有无。
- **摘录/笔记**：
  > "the 'technological real economy' is a collectively autocatalytic set."
  > "All the production functions are catalyzed by human hands, minds or products of the economic web itself. The entire system is a collectively autocatalytic set."
  > "Some economies are supracritical, like the global economy...Some economies are subcritical, producing no or few new goods or services."
  > (Hordijk et al. 2023): "product transformation networks resulting from the combinatorial TAP model have a high probability of containing autocatalytic (RAF) sets"
  > "These initial results strongly support earlier claims that the economy can indeed be seen as an autocatalytic set"
