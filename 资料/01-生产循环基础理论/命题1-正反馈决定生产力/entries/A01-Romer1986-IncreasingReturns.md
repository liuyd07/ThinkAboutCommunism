# Increasing Returns and Long-Run Growth
- **作者**：Paul M. Romer
- **出处**：Journal of Political Economy, vol. 94, no. 5, pp. 1002-1037
- **年份**：1986
- **DOI**：10.1086/261420
- **核心观点**：在完全竞争均衡模型中引入知识作为具有递增边际生产力的投入要素，证明人均产出可以在无界增长，且增长率可能单调递增。长期增长主要受追求利润最大化的前瞻性行为主体积累知识所驱动。这是内生增长理论的奠基之作。
- **与命题的关联**：正面论证核心文献。Romer 证明递增报酬（而非外生技术进步）是长期增长的驱动力，这与命题"正反馈决定生产力"高度一致——知识的非竞争性和溢出效应创造了生产正循环。
- **摘录/笔记**：
  > "This paper presents a fully specified model of long-run growth in which knowledge is assumed to be an input in production that has increasing marginal productivity."
  > "per capita output can grow without bound, possibly at a rate that is monotonically increasing over time."
  > "long-run growth is driven primarily by the accumulation of knowledge by forward-looking, profit-maximizing agents."
  > "In contrast to models based on diminishing returns, growth rates can be increasing over time, the effects of small disturbances can be amplified by the actions of private agents."
