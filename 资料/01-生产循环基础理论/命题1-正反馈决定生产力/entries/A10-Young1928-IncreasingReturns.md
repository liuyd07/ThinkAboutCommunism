# Increasing Returns and Economic Progress
- **作者**：Allyn A. Young
- **出处**：The Economic Journal, vol. 38, no. 152, pp. 527-542
- **年份**：1928
- **DOI**：-
- **核心观点**：递增报酬的机制不能通过观察单个企业或行业规模变化来理解，因为产业的逐步分工和专业化是递增报酬实现的核心过程。分工依赖于市场范围，而市场范围又依赖于分工——这种相互依赖关系是经济进步的可能路径（在获得新知识带来的进步之外）。这篇论文是杨格定理（Young's Theorem）的原始出处。
- **与命题的关联**：正面论证经典文献。Young 揭示的"分工依赖于市场范围，市场范围依赖于分工"正是生产循环中正反馈结构的经典表述。三个要点中第三点直接描述了正循环：分工↔市场范围的相互依赖性。
- **摘录/笔记**：
  > "First, the mechanism of increasing returns is not to be discerned adequately by observing the effects of variations in the size of an individual firm or of a particular industry, for the progressive division and specialisation of industries is an essential part of the process by which increasing returns are realised."
  > "Second, the securing of increasing returns depends upon the progressive division of labour, and the principal economies of the division of labour, in its modern forms, are the economies which are to be had by using labour in roundabout or indirect ways."
  > "Third, the division of labour depends upon the extent of the market, but the extent of the market also depends upon the division of labour. In this circumstance lies the possibility of economic progress, apart from the progress which comes as a result of the new knowledge."
