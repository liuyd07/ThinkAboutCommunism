# P19 材料清单

1. IMF, Easterly & Fischer, *What We Can Learn from the Soviet Collapse*：苏联 1960—1987 年相对于投资与教育率的增长表现极差，关键问题是体系刚性与投资回报快速下降，而非投资率本身。https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/022/0031/004/article-A001-en.xml
2. IMF, *How Large Was the Output Collapse in Russia?*：计划体系重总产值、新建轻维护与配送；未完工程和未安装设备相对固定投资上升，提供物质能力层面的反例。https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/001/1994/154/article-A001-en.xml
3. OECD, *Guidelines on Corporate Governance of State-Owned Enterprises 2024*：国企问责存在经理、董事会、所有权机构、部委、政府和议会的复杂代理链；透明、评价和政策一致性是必要条件。https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-on-corporate-governance-of-state-owned-enterprises-2024_18a24f43-en/full-report/component-3.html
4. OECD 国企指南实施回顾：公共服务义务与商业活动应分账，公共目标的成本须单独核算；这防止把亏损、补贴和公共收益混成一个财务指标。https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2020/03/implementing-the-oecd-guidelines-on-corporate-governance-of-state-owned-enterprises-review-of-recent-developments_e5f010cd/4caa0c3b-en.pdf
5. NIST, *AI Risk Management Framework 1.0*：要求全生命周期治理、测量、管理，明确人机责任、持续监测和安全退役；AI 的可信度不能由自动化程度推出。https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/nist.ai.100-1.pdf
6. NIST 人机交互附录：模型把复杂社会现象转换为量化表示会损失情境，偏见和不透明性可能被放大；人是否有权和有激励挑战模型必须明确。https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/appendices/app-c-ai-risk-management-and-human-ai-interaction/
